Who am I?

Hi! I'm Kate Olguin (any pronouns), a game designer from the Boston area and a board member for Boston Game Dev Inc, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit focused on supporting the Boston area game development community. I'm also on the 2025 Forbes 30 Under 30 list for games!

I love making different worlds and creating the people, places, and creatures that live in them. I'm all about making games to make people feel less alone, or to give players a laugh or two. I also love helping people learn how to get started in game development, or level up their game dev skills. For me, making games has been a dream come true, and I want to empower others to achieve that same dream!


I'm always on the lookout for interesting new projects, and I love to chat with all sorts of folks! Drop me a line!
