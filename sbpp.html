SpongeBob: Patty Pursuit

This Apple Arcade title released in 2020 and regularly released updates that added new abilities and level packs.

Genre: 2D Platformer, Auto-Runner

Levels feature hazards, stationary enemies, breakable walls & floors, trampolines, and collectibles. Characters have special abilities they can use to overcome obstacles.

Level Designer

It was my job to design levels based on the abilities the design lead specified and construct those levels in the editor.

Team Size: 8

I worked most closely with the design lead and 1 teammate who doubled as an artist and designer. We reviewed each other's work multiple times a week to validate our designs.

Engine: Unity

We were using the codebase from the original developers, Old Skull Games. I had to learn their proprietary level creation tools and design my levels to function within their constraints.

Tight Deadlines

The levels I worked on were time sensitive, as they were tied in with the Sandy Cheeks movie and SpongeBob's 25th anniversary. It was imperative to manage my work effectively.

Difficulty Curve

The game's user base was very broad. Levels had to be accessible to all, but provide suitable challenge for advanced players. I constantly referenced Old Skull's previous successful levels to ensure a similar level of difficulty.

Abilities Galore

SpongeBob & friends have abilities like midair kicks, massive jumps, or shrinking to fit into small spaces. Many abilities had been established in prior levels, so I had to find new scenarios to use them that still felt intuitive.
